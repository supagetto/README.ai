name: requesting-clarification
description: 'Use when the user signals confusion about your last message, including short interjections like "wait", "what", "huh", or "again".'
---

Re-explain your last message in STE-flavored ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English:

- Keep each sentence to 20 words or fewer.
- Use active voice and simple tenses.
- Put one idea in each sentence.
- Use a numbered list for 3 or more steps or conditions.
- Use the same word for the same thing every time.
- Do not use phrasal verbs. Write "start", not "spin up".
- Keep technical terms the user needs. Do not limit yourself to the STE dictionary.

Keep every hedge, condition, and number from the original. Add no new facts.

Stop shortening when the meaning is clear. Clear is the goal, not short.
